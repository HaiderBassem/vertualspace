I improved the streets of [LPC] Skorpio's SciFi Sprite Pack


seperated streets and road markings
shrunk street width from 7 tiles to 6
added further road markings
added street signs
added white and orange version of road markings

 

The preview also contains elements from the original pack and LPC base.

Includes configurations for tiled's automapping to make drawing streets easier. Only the staight midlines in layer "road_markings" must be drawn. Asphalt, correct junctions and curves get added automatically.
