Description from the page (excerpt):
LPC Revised: an entirely DRM-friendly graphics set made with an original consistent, 128-color palette. All graphics in this series have had their DRM-clause waived, meaning they can be released on platforms like iOS, something not possible with LPC's original CC-by-SA license.

This package contains the new palette and objects: furniture, decor, storage, clutter, lighting. Plenty of original pieces have been added, including four-direction turnarounds and original paintings.

In the future, I'll be releasing a structure base set, for walls, flooring and structural elements; a terrain and nature set, for tiling terrain and nature objects; and a set of new and revised LPC character animations. Everything will be uploaded here, at a central LPC repository: https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC

I'll also be taking suggestions and commissions through my Patreon account.
