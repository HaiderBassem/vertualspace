Description from the page (excerpt):
Four loopable rain SFX, with a length varying between 25 and 45 seconds. Recorded about two hours ago with a single mono mic at my window, then I processed it a bit, trying to make it sound stereo. It was more an experiment, but it can probably be useful anyway ;)

 

Available in two formats, the samples are the same :

- MP3, 192 kbps, constant bitrate

- OGG, 160 kbps
