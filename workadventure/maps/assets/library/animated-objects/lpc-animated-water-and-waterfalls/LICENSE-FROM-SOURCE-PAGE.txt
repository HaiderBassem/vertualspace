Description from the page (excerpt):
I made animated water tiles and waterfall tiles using LPC set for my RPG Game Maker (http://peterlzy.wix.com/rpgaction)

The water tiles are meant to be translucent over LPC rock or LPC sand tiles.  I have merged the layers to optimize performance on the ipad/iphone.

The water tiles also have 3 different glistening effects to create variety.
